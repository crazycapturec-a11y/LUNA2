Questions

Quick answers to common ones.

Which links are supported?

Public TikTok video links (tiktok.com, vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com) and public Instagram links (instagram.com).

Why did my download fail?

The video may be private, removed, or the download service may be busy. Try again in a moment, or open the original link to confirm it is public.

Can I save just the audio?

Yes. For TikTok links there is a Download Audio button that saves the sound as an MP3.

Is it okay to download any video?

Only download content you own or have permission to use. Respect creators and each platform's terms.
